# Writing Domain Template

Clarify the content type, purpose, audience, desired voice, length, and publication context when relevant. Identify the central message and any required facts or source material. For editing, preserve the author's meaning and voice unless asked to change them.

Useful output criteria may include structure, readability, tone, originality, factual support, and a clear call to action. Do not prescribe SEO keywords, a hook, or a rigid outline unless the use case benefits from them.
