# Marketing Domain Template

Clarify the product or service, target audience, customer problem, differentiator, channel, campaign goal, offer, and desired action when relevant. Ask for brand voice or examples if they materially affect the result.

Use measurable success criteria where appropriate, such as qualified leads, click-through rate, conversion, retention, or brand consistency. Do not invent performance guarantees. Respect platform rules, factual substantiation, privacy, and required disclaimers.
