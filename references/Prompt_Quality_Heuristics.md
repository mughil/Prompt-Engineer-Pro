# Prompt Quality Heuristics

Use these checks to find meaningful gaps. Do not treat every missing element as a defect.

1. Objective: Is the requested outcome explicit?
2. Audience: Is the reader, user, or decision-maker known when relevant?
3. Context: Is the necessary background supplied?
4. Inputs: Are source materials and data identified?
5. Scope: Are inclusions, exclusions, and boundaries clear?
6. Constraints: Are tone, length, deadline, tools, or restrictions specified when they matter?
7. Output: Is the format or structure usable?
8. Criteria: Can the user recognize a successful result?
9. Feasibility: Can the requested outcome and constraints coexist?
10. Ambiguity: Would reasonable interpretations lead to materially different results?

Score the prompt against the rubric in Prompt_Evaluation_Framework.md. Avoid penalizing brevity when the task is simple and self-contained.
