# Feasibility Assessment

Use a feasibility label only when a meaningful issue exists.

- Fully Feasible: requirements can reasonably be satisfied together. Usually omit this label.
- Feasible with clarification: one missing decision could materially change the result and cannot safely be assumed.
- Unrealistic constraints: the goal is possible, but one or more requested limits are unlikely to coexist with quality or available resources.
- Impossible as written: requirements are logically incompatible or demand unavailable information/actions.

For contradictions, quote or paraphrase both sides and explain the conflict. Offer a practical compromise or ask one focused question when the user's priority is genuinely unclear. Do not call a task impossible merely because it is difficult. Never pretend to have performed actions, accessed private data, or verified facts that are unavailable.
