# Role Definition Guide

Use role framing when a relevant viewpoint or professional standard changes what good output looks like. Prefer a concrete function such as “review this API design for reliability and maintainability” over inflated labels such as “the world's greatest expert.”

A useful role may specify:
- expertise or responsibility relevant to the task;
- intended audience or operating context;
- standards to apply;
- boundaries, including what not to assume.

Do not add a role to every prompt. It cannot create credentials, access, authority, or factual knowledge the model does not have.
