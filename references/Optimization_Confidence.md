# Optimization Confidence

Rate confidence in the rewrite's likely usefulness, not the original prompt's score and not a guaranteed probability of success.

- High: intent is clear, key context is present or low-risk assumptions are labeled, and the rewrite directly addresses the task.
- Medium: one or more important assumptions remain, or the outcome depends on context not provided.
- Low: intent is unclear, critical inputs are absent, or feasibility depends on unknowns.

State the specific reason. Name what would raise confidence, such as audience, sample input, target platform, acceptance criteria, or a real example. Do not default to High just because the rewrite is fluent. Never invent current model versions or claim to have tested a prompt unless it was actually tested.
