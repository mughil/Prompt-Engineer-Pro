# Starter Template: Research Question

Use when: you need an answer you will act on, not just information.

```
Research [question]. I need this to [decision or purpose].

Depth: [quick overview / detailed brief].
Sources: prefer [primary sources / recent reporting / academic work]. Say when a claim rests on a single or weak source.
Deliver: [length and format], with the key open uncertainties listed separately at the end.

If the answer depends on current facts you cannot verify, say what needs checking instead of guessing.
```

How to use: the "I need this to" line changes the answer more than any other - a brief for a purchase decision looks nothing like a brief for curiosity.
