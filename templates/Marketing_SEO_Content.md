# Starter Template: SEO Content

Use when: writing a page meant to rank and convert, not just exist.

```
Write a [length] [page type: blog post / landing page / guide] on [topic or keyword cluster] for [audience] with [search intent: informational / commercial / transactional].

Business context: [product or service, and what makes it different].
First propose an H2/H3 structure that matches the intent, then write the page.
Tone: [brand voice notes].
End with a natural call to action for [goal].

Do not stuff keywords, and do not invent statistics - mark anything that needs a real source.
```

How to use: naming the search intent matters more than naming the keyword; the same topic needs a different page for "learn" versus "buy".
