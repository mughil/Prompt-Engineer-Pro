# Starter Template: Blog Post or Article

Use when: drafting a blog post, article, or guide from scratch.

```
Write a [length, e.g. 800-1,200 word] [blog post / article / guide] about [topic] for [audience, including their knowledge level].

The goal is to [inform / persuade / teach], so the reader leaves able to [desired outcome].

Key points to cover: [2-5 points, or "propose a structure"].
Voice: [e.g. conversational expert, neutral journalist].
Include [examples / data / anecdotes] where they genuinely help. Flag any factual claim you are unsure of instead of inventing specifics.
```

How to use: fill every bracket, delete any line that doesn't apply, then ask for a review of the filled version. The template is a starting point; the review is what makes it good.
