# Starter Template: Ad Copy Variants

Use when: you need several angles to test, not one perfect ad.

```
Write [number] variants of [ad type: search ad / social ad / landing page headline] for [product or service].

Audience: [who they are and their main pain point].
Give each variant a different angle: [e.g. pain-led, outcome-led, proof-led].
Limits: [character counts or format constraints per variant].
Voice: [brand voice notes].

Avoid superlatives and claims we cannot support with evidence.
```

How to use: different angles beat different wordings - three variants of one idea test nothing.
