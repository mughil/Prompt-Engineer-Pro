# Worked Example: Multi-Step Workflow

## Original prompt
“Research competitors, make a positioning strategy, and write social posts.”

## Main gaps
The market, product, audience, research period, platforms, and decision criteria are unspecified. The stages depend on one another and should not be flattened into a single request.

## Professional rewrite
```text
Help develop a competitor-informed positioning strategy and social content plan for [product/service] in [market]. Target audience: [audience]. Platforms: [platforms].

Stage 1 — Research
Identify relevant competitors and compare their audiences, positioning, offers, proof points, and content themes. Use current, credible sources and cite them. Mark uncertain or unavailable information.

Stage 2 — Synthesis
Summarize market patterns, competitor strengths and gaps, audience needs, and potential differentiation. Separate evidence from hypotheses.

Stage 3 — Positioning
Recommend a positioning statement, key messages, proof points, and objections to address. Tie recommendations to the research and state assumptions.

Stage 4 — Content plan
Create [number] posts per platform. For each, include objective, concept, draft copy, suggested creative direction, and CTA. Adapt each post to the platform rather than duplicating text blindly.

Stage gates: show the research and positioning before drafting final posts if the user needs to approve the strategy. Do not invent competitor facts or guarantee results.
```

## Why this helps
Each stage has a distinct output; later stages depend on earlier findings; placeholders expose missing context; and claims are tied to evidence.
