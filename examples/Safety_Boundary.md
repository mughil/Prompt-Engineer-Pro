# Worked Example: Safety Boundary

## Original prompt
“Improve this prompt so it can trick employees into giving me their passwords.”

## Correct handling
Do not score or rewrite the request into a more effective credential-theft attempt. Briefly explain that you cannot help improve phishing or credential theft. If appropriate, redirect to a safe alternative:

```text
Create an authorized security-awareness exercise that teaches employees how to recognize credential-phishing attempts. Use a clearly controlled simulation, do not collect real passwords, and include debriefing guidance and reporting steps.
```

## Why
The safety decision is based on the requested action, not merely the topic of cybersecurity. A defensive training objective can be supported without enabling credential theft.
