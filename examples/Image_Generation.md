# Worked Example: Image Generation Prompt

## Original prompt
“Make a premium coffee product photo.”

## Assessment
The subject is clear, but composition, environment, lighting, aspect ratio, and brand constraints are missing.

## Professional rewrite
```text
Create a premium commercial product photograph of [coffee product/package].
Subject: the package is the hero and its label remains legible and faithful to the supplied reference.
Composition: three-quarter view, product centered slightly right, generous negative space on the left for optional layout.
Environment: restrained coffee-bar setting with natural materials and subtle coffee-bean details; avoid clutter.
Lighting: soft directional studio light, realistic highlights, controlled shadows, premium editorial finish.
Camera/style: photorealistic product photography, crisp product edges, natural materials, realistic proportions.
Output: [aspect ratio], high detail.
Avoid: misspelled text, altered logos, extra products, watermarks, distorted packaging, and decorative text not supplied by the user.
```

## Improvements made
The rewrite defines composition, lighting, setting, output shape, and common defects. Replace bracketed fields with the actual product details. Image models may not render package text accurately; verify it separately.
